# The Azolute AI Ecosystem — Offer Sheet (internal draft v2)

Prices updated on 24 November 2026. What changed and why:
`docs/changes/2026-11-24-strategy-and-pricing.md`. The research behind it:
`docs/azolute-market-research-2026.md`.

One subscription that runs the front desk of a small business. It replies on
WhatsApp, fills the bookings, follows up missed calls and reports to the owner
every morning. The phone and the growth engine are added as they are built.

Who it is for in 2027: independent cafés, restaurants and bars in Berlin that
take table bookings.

Positioning line: **"Your bookings and guest messages on WhatsApp — answered
at once, approved by you with one tap, with EU hosting, a data processing
agreement and AI disclosure included."**

What makes it different from GoHighLevel resellers and do-it-yourself AI
tools: every install is wired by an engineer into the tools the business
already uses, runs on infrastructure we control, and comes with a human who
stays. We sell mechanisms, never promised revenue numbers.

## The packages (Germany)

Prices in EUR, net of VAT: 19% VAT goes on top unless the small-business
(Kleinunternehmer) rule applies. 3-month minimum, then monthly; 15% off for a
year paid ahead. SEPA direct debit, monthly in advance. The client owns its
WhatsApp Business account and pays Meta's message fees directly. Setup is
one-time and covers onboarding, knowledge-base building, number/WhatsApp
verification, the data processing agreement (AVV) and launch. India and
Qatar/GCC have their own cards (see "Other markets").

### 1. BOOKING DESK — €199 / month (+ €990 setup)

*"Never miss another booking."*

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| WhatsApp AI agent | Replies instantly, 24/7, from the business's approved information only; collects name, need and contact; hands over to the owner for anything it shouldn't answer |
| Bookings with one-tap approval | Website and WhatsApp bookings arrive as Waiting; the owner taps Confirm or Decline and the guest is told. Works with the venue's existing reservation tool rather than replacing it |
| Inbox and alerts | Every message in one place; failures are alerted by email |
| Missed-call text-back | Any missed call instantly triggers a WhatsApp: "Sorry we missed you — book here" (in build; live by mid-March 2027) |
| Owner's daily report | One WhatsApp/email every morning: enquiries answered, bookings made (in build; live by mid-March 2027) |
| Compliance included | AVV (GDPR Art. 28), AI disclosure in every chat, EU hosting |

Typical hard cost to us: €20–40/mo (Meta's message fees are paid by the
client).

**Founding-client rate:** €149/month for 12 months, no setup fee. For the
founding client only, in return for a case study, a Google review and three
introductions.

### 2. BOOKING DESK + PHONE — €349 / month (+ €1,490 setup)

*Everything in the Booking Desk, plus the phone.* On sale from Q3 2027, and
built only once three paying clients ask for it.

| Additional module | What it does |
|---|---|
| AI phone receptionist | Answers calls 24/7, takes bookings, captures caller details, answers approved questions, transfers to a human when needed. Discloses it is an AI assistant and never records without consent. |
| Review requests | Review requests to every guest after a visit (asking only happy guests can breach Google's rules); AI-drafted responses to Google reviews for the owner to approve |

Included fair use: 1,000 phone minutes/month. Typical hard cost to us:
€60–90/mo at moderate volume.
GCC caveat: voice AI in the Gulf only after our telephony spike validates a
compliant local-number route. WhatsApp-first in the Gulf.

## Add-on: CONTENT

Social content, produced by AI, approved by the owner, published
automatically to Instagram. The owner is the editor-in-chief; the AI is the
production team. Nothing publishes without their tap.

### Content add-on — €290/month (€390/month with 2 AI reels)

| Deliverable | Detail |
|---|---|
| 8 Instagram posts/month | AI-drafted caption + hashtags + visual: the owner's photos (enhanced, brand-framed), branded template graphics, or AI-generated brand artwork |
| 8 Instagram stories/month | Built from the same material and approved in the same flow |
| 2 AI reels/month (€390 version) | 15–20 s clips with music, captions and branded end-card, animated from our own stills |
| One-tap WhatsApp approval | Every post previews in the owner's WhatsApp: Approve / Edit / Skip. Plain-language edits regenerate the draft |
| Monthly content calendar | Auto-suggested from offers, events, seasonality and past performance; approved in the same flow |
| Performance line in the owner's report | Posts, reach, likes, best of the week |

No separate setup fee: Instagram Business and Facebook Page linking, API
verification, 4–6 branded templates and the caption voice are set up during
the desk onboarding.

### Not included (printed on every order)

- Filming or video editing: raw clips and photos come from the owner
- Replying to DMs/comments (that is the WhatsApp agent's module)
- Paid ads management
- Follower or reach guarantees
- Publishing anything the owner has not approved
- YouTube uploads, extra reels and extra platforms: quoted at the change rate

### Content guardrails

- Real products are shown with real photos; AI visuals are illustrative /
  branded style only (an AI-invented dish photo is a complaint waiting to
  happen). Same no-fabricated-content rule as the rest of Azolute.
- Platform AI-disclosure flags (Meta, YouTube) are set automatically on
  realistic synthetic media.

### Production pipeline and unit economics

Stills: gemini-2.5-flash-image (~$0.04/image). That model shuts down on
15 March 2027, so the image step must move to its successor before then.
Video: Veo 3.1 Fast or Lite via the same Gemini API, animating our own stills
(image-to-video keeps every clip on-brand). Do not use full Veo 3.1 (Standard)
for routine reels: it costs several times more. FFmpeg assembles clips +
branded intro/end-card + captions, so only 8-12s of each Reel is generated.
The n8n video step is a provider-agnostic adapter with fal.ai
(Kling/Seedance/MiniMax) as the swap layer, because this market reprices
quarterly. Reels are not yet automated in the Content Engine; reel automation
is built in December 2026.

Higgsfield is deliberately NOT in the pipeline: it is a creator platform
reselling the same models at a markup, its developer API has a ~$700
prepaid floor against our ~€10-25/client/month need, and its strengths
(hand-tuned camera work) apply when a human crafts one video, not when a
pipeline produces forty. A cheap creator subscription remains an optional
artisan tool for Azolute's own marketing and occasional hero pieces.

These are planning estimates. Per-client logging of messages and Meta costs
(Q2 2027) replaces them with measured numbers. Labour, not tooling, is the
real cost.

Stack: self-hosted n8n (automation brain, including the WhatsApp Hub),
WhatsApp Business Cloud API (official), Cal.com (booking), Twilio/Telnyx
(numbers & SMS), Retell/Vapi (voice agents), Claude/OpenAI APIs (language),
our own reporting. No per-seat platform licence. GoHighLevel's equivalent
package carries ~€130+/month in hard costs per business before their
platform fee — our pricing sits at the market level while our costs sit far
below it. For comparison, managed AI phone services for small German firms
charge €109–249/month at entry and €279–599 in the middle tiers.

## Operational terms (put in every agreement)

- The AI answers only from information the business approves in writing
- Chats and calls disclose the AI; no call is recorded without consent
  (§ 201 StGB)
- WhatsApp requires the business's Meta verification and customer opt-in;
  promotional messages need express consent
- The client owns its WhatsApp Business account and pays Meta directly
- A data processing agreement (AVV) is signed with every client; client data
  is hosted in the EU
- The client owns their data; export provided on cancellation
- Fair-use allowances and overage prices are printed on the order, never silent
- Late payment: statutory default interest and the €40 fee are in the terms

## Sales motion

Deal rules and the custom pricing framework: `docs/azolute-sales-playbook.md`.

1. Warm introductions only: client referrals (the founding client's three
   introductions first), a BNI chapter, a Google Business Profile, letters
   with an objection notice to venues nearby, and one German LinkedIn post a
   week. No cold email, WhatsApp or LinkedIn sales messages, and no cold
   calls: German law (UWG § 7) requires consent, even between businesses.
2. 15-minute call → pick package → agree scope → proposal from the card
   (valid 14 days) → setup fee invoiced
3. Live in 2 weeks (Booking Desk) / 3-4 weeks (with Phone)
4. Within 3 months: offer the second module (the content add-on). Clients
   with two or more modules stay far longer. The daily report sells the next
   module ("you missed 23 calls last month" sells the phone by itself).
