# AZOLUTE AI — SALES PLAYBOOK

Lessons encoded from sales roleplays and the business plan. The goal:
customise confidently without ever selling something that costs more to
deliver than the customer pays.

---

## The one rule above all others

**Never quote a price during a discovery call.**

Discovery diagnoses. The proposal (sent within 48 hours, from the
standardised framework below) is where prices live. If pressed on a call:
"Our packages start at €149 a month; I'll send you an exact proposal
tomorrow once I understand your setup."

---

## The four-stage conversation

| Stage | Objective | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discovery | Understand the business, budget and problems | Completed qualification checklist |
| 2. Diagnosis | Identify the single biggest obstacle to revenue | One named problem the customer agrees with |
| 3. Recommendation | Explain the most suitable package and why | Customer understands the "why" before any price |
| 4. Commercial proposal | Scope, price, timeline, success metrics | Written proposal from the pricing framework |

The customer must understand why we recommend a service before we discuss
what it costs. Advice first builds the trust that selling first destroys.

## Qualification checklists

### Service businesses (salons, clinics, restaurants, studios)

- How do customers book today? Phone / walk-in / DM / online?
- Call volume and when calls are missed
- Tools in use (calendar, POS, WhatsApp, spreadsheets)
- Staff count and who answers enquiries
- Repeat-customer share, any retention efforts
- Languages customers use
- Budget range and decision maker

### E-commerce businesses

Do NOT discuss marketing before these are known:

- Which countries do they sell in?
- Average order value and gross margin per order
- Inventory availability and depth
- Website platform and checkout system (Shopify, Woo, custom?)
- Shipping and returns capability
- Planned launch date (if pre-launch)
- Existing audience (email list, social, zero?)

Traffic without fulfilment capability is a wasted spend: there is no value
in generating a thousand visits for a store that cannot ship.

## Success metrics (agree them in the proposal, report on them monthly)

| Service businesses | E-commerce businesses |
|---|---|
| Calls answered / missed-call recoveries | Website visitors |
| Bookings made (by channel) | Email/WhatsApp subscribers captured |
| No-show rate | Add-to-cart activity |
| Enquiry response time | Checkout completion rate |
| Repeat-visit rate | Cost per purchase |
| Review count and rating | Abandoned-cart recovery rate |

Without agreed metrics, the value of the service cannot be demonstrated,
and renewal conversations become opinion contests.

## Custom pricing framework

When a need does not fit a catalogue package, the quote is BUILT, never
improvised:

1. Estimate delivery hours (onboarding and monthly separately)
2. Labour at the EXTERNAL rate of €45/hour minimum
   (the €25/hour figure in the business plan is internal founder
   compensation, not a charge-out rate; quoting external work at internal
   cost guarantees losses)
3. Infrastructure and API costs × 1.3 safety factor
4. Check against minimums: no subscription below €149/month, no one-time
   project below €490, no engagement below 60% gross margin
5. Round to a clean price and write the deliverables and allowances on
   the order

Manual work is priced by labour, always. The Content Engine prices
(€99/€229) are only valid because production is automated with owner
approval; the moment a package includes manual filming, editing or
design hours, it leaves the catalogue and goes through this framework.

## Two catalogues, not one

The Starter/Growth/Full packages are designed for appointment-based
service businesses. E-commerce has different mechanics:

| Service businesses | E-commerce equivalent |
|---|---|
| Appointment bookings | Product purchases |
| Missed-call recovery | Abandoned-cart recovery |
| Appointment reminders | Order notifications |
| Customer retention offers | Repeat-purchase campaigns |
| AI receptionist | AI shopping assistant |
| No-show prevention | Checkout conversion improvements |

Do not force an e-commerce customer into the service catalogue; use the
e-commerce track below.

### E-COMMERCE TRACK (DRAFT — validate delivery costs before selling)

track actual hours and infrastructure, then fix the prices.

**Commerce Starter — target €199/month (+ €690 setup)**

**Commerce Growth — target €349/month (+ €990 setup)**

  purchase)

Content Engine (Lite €99 / Pro €229) attaches to either track unchanged:
it is automated production and its economics do not depend on the
business type.
